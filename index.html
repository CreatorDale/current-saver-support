Frequently Asked Questions

How does bill scanning work?

Tap the scan icon on the Bills tab and take a photo (or choose one from your library). Current Saver reads the total cost, kWh used, and billing dates directly on your device using Apple's Vision framework — nothing is uploaded anywhere. You'll always get a chance to review and correct the numbers before saving.

Does my data leave my phone?

No. Your bills, appliances, and everything you enter are stored locally on your device. Weather and regional rate comparisons make requests to Apple's WeatherKit and standard mapping services using only your location — nothing personally identifying is sent, and nothing about your bills or usage is ever transmitted anywhere.

How do I connect a smart plug?

When adding or editing an appliance, turn on "Get live wattage from a smart plug" and enter its local IP address. Current Saver currently supports Shelly-brand smart plugs over your home Wi-Fi network — no cloud account required.

Why does my rate look higher than the average shown?

Your average rate is calculated as total bill cost ÷ kWh used, so it naturally includes every charge on your bill — energy, distribution, fees, and surcharges. If your household uses less electricity than average, fixed charges get spread across fewer kWh, which can push your blended rate above the reference average even if you're not being overcharged. The Savings tab explains this when it applies to you.

I found a bug or have a feature request — where do I send it?
